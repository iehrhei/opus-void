# OPUS.VOID

*floating structures of sound · 一座可以被聆听的建筑*

An interactive, hand-inked cube of musical staves. Each of its six faces is a full page of score and a different material of sound — pure, glass, droplet, breath, distance, felt. Ink notes onto any face, then play them all at once and watch the lines between them build an architecture of sound.

一个由手绘五线谱构成的立方体。六个面各是一整页乐谱，也各是一种声音的材质——纯音、玻璃、水滴、气息、远方、毡。在任意一面落下音符，让它们一同奏响，看音符之间的连线搭起一座声音建筑。

**Live:** https://iehrhei.github.io/opus-void/

- **click** a staff to ink a note · click again to erase · **shift** for sharp
- **double-click** a face to write up close · ← → ↑ ↓ to move between faces · **esc** back
- **space** to play · **V** to enter the void · **⌘Z** / **⇧⌘Z** undo / redo
- **share** — sign it, add a line for someone, and send a link or a poster with a QR code. The whole structure travels inside the link.
- English / 中文 toggle in the top-right corner.

An Ouvroir de Rhei experiment. Built with Three.js and the Web Audio API; no build step, no server.

Fonts: Cormorant Garamond, Homemade Apple, Noto Music, Noto Serif SC and LXGW WenKai (subset), all under the SIL Open Font License. Three.js (MIT), qrcode-generator by Kazuhiko Arase (MIT).
